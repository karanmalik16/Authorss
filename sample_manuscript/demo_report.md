## Continuity Audit Flags

### ⚠️ [HIGH] Contradictory Eye Color: Lady Vivienne (PENDING)
**Explanation:** In Chapter 1: The Midnight Crypt, Lady Vivienne's eyes were established as 'emerald'. In this passage, they are described as 'violet'.

| Earlier Evidence (Ch 1: Chapter 1: The Midnight Crypt, Sent #9) | Current Conflict (Ch 2: Chapter 2: Council of Whispers, Sent #5) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Lady Vivienne stepped forward from the shadows, her striking emerald-green eyes narrowing in the torchlight." | "Lady Vivienne turned her piercing violet eyes toward the council table, watching the nervous fidgeting of the noble delegates." |

- **Author Fix Suggestion:** Change 'violet' back to 'emerald' or explain the change (e.g. illusion, contacts, lighting).

### ⚠️ [HIGH] Spatiotemporal Impossibility: Travel to Ashen Fortress (PENDING)
**Explanation:** In Chapter 1, traveling to Ashen Fortress was established as 'three-day hard ride'. In this passage, a character travels there and returns in a single afternoon without magical teleportation.

| Earlier Evidence (Ch 1: Chapter 1: The Midnight Crypt, Sent #18) | Current Conflict (Ch 2: Chapter 2: Council of Whispers, Sent #8) |
| :--- | :--- |
| ""The Ashen Fortress is a grueling three-day hard ride north through the Jagged Peaks." | "Sir Justinian arrived breathless, having ridden to the Ashen Fortress and returned that very afternoon to deliver the northern reports." |

- **Author Fix Suggestion:** Adjust the timeline to account for a week of travel, or have the message carried by carrier raven or magic mirror.

### ⚠️ [HIGH] Epistemic Leak: The Court Knows Undisclosed Secret (PENDING)
**Explanation:** The Court was not present when the secret was revealed in Chapter 1 and was strictly kept in the dark ('sworn to absolute secrecy'). Yet here, The Court discloses the exact details without any intermediate scene explaining how they learned it.

| Earlier Evidence (Ch 1: Chapter 1: The Midnight Crypt, Sent #1) | Current Conflict (Ch 2: Chapter 2: Council of Whispers, Sent #14) |
| :--- | :--- |
| ""The Archduke was strangled in his study at midnight with a crimson silken cord," Vaelen whispered solemnly." | ""The court fools think the Duke fell ill, but everyone knows the Archduke was strangled with a crimson cord in his bed!"" |

- **Author Fix Suggestion:** Either insert a scene showing how The Court discovered the secret, or change their dialogue so they remain unaware.

### ⚠️ [HIGH] Physical Impossibility: Master Corvus (Severed Right Hand) (PENDING)
**Explanation:** In Chapter 1, Master Corvus's right hand was established as severed/lost at the wrist. Here, Master Corvus is described performing a physical two-handed action using their right fingers.

| Earlier Evidence (Ch 1: Chapter 1: The Midnight Crypt, Sent #14) | Current Conflict (Ch 3: Chapter 3: The River Crossing, Sent #5) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Three years had passed since the bloody Siege of Oakhaven, where Corvus had his right hand severed at the wrist by a rebel's claymore." | "Master Corvus gripped the heavy greatsword with both hands, his white-knuckled right fingers tightening on the leather hilt as he prepared to strike." |

- **Author Fix Suggestion:** Describe Master Corvus bracing with their left hand or silver cuff prosthetic instead of using right fingers.

### ⚠️ [HIGH] World Rule Violation: Shadow-iron in Running Water (PENDING)
**Explanation:** In Chapter 1, the magical rule was established that shadow-iron blades dissolve into vapor the instant they touch running water. Here, a character wades through a rushing river with shadow-iron blades drawn.

| Earlier Evidence (Ch 1: Chapter 1: The Midnight Crypt, Sent #24) | Current Conflict (Ch 3: Chapter 3: The River Crossing, Sent #8) |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Remember the ancient rule: shadow-iron dissolves into harmless vapor the instant it touches running water." | "The assassin drew twin shadow-iron daggers, leaped into the rushing waters of the River Marrow, and waded across the torrent with blades drawn, entirely undeterred by the churning spray." |

- **Author Fix Suggestion:** Have the blades dissolve immediately, or replace the weapons with ordinary steel daggers.

# Chapter 2: Council of Whispers

The high council chamber of Sunspire was filled with uneasy murmurs. Heavy rain drummed against the stained glass windows as the lords and commanders took their seats around the grand oak table.

Lady Vivienne entered quietly and took her seat at the high table. Lady Vivienne turned her piercing violet eyes toward the council table, watching the nervous fidgeting of the noble delegates. She rested her slender fingers upon the polished wood, observing every twitch and whisper.

Suddenly, the heavy oak doors banged open. Sir Justinian arrived breathless, having ridden to the Ashen Fortress and returned that very afternoon to deliver the northern reports. The messenger bowed deeply, his cape drenched from the storm, claiming he had traversed the entire mountain range without sleeping.

Lord Ronald stood up abruptly from the western bench. He had returned from three months guarding the eastern marches, entirely absent from the crypt. "Why are we pretending there is order?" Lord Ronald shouted, his face red with indignation. "The court thinks the Duke fell to sudden illness, but I know the truth—the Archduke was strangled with a crimson cord in his bed!"

Captain Vaelen's jaw clenched. A chill swept through the hall as Master Corvus leaned forward, staring at Ronald with suspicion burning in his dark eyes. How could Ronald possibly know the classified details of the murder?

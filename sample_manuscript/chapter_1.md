# Chapter 1: The Midnight Crypt

The torchlight flickered against the damp limestone walls of the ancient crypt. Captain Vaelen knelt beside the altar, his fingers trembling as he examined the crimson silken cord still tangled in the dead Archduke's cloak.

"The Archduke was strangled in his study at midnight with a crimson silken cord," Vaelen whispered solemnly. He stood up, turning to face his two companions. "I swear you both to absolute secrecy. No other soul in the realm knows how or when the Duke died. If the rebellion learns of this before the coronation, the city will burn."

Lady Vivienne stepped forward from the shadows, her striking emerald-green eyes narrowing in the torchlight. She adjusted her bow across her shoulder, a master archer who favored her right hand in every skirmish. "The secret stays with us, Captain. Not even my brother shall hear a word."

Behind her stood Master Corvus, the high inquisitor. Three years had passed since the bloody Siege of Oakhaven, where Corvus had his right hand severed at the wrist by a rebel's claymore. Now, a polished silver cuff covered the stump, gleam reflecting cold off his dark vestments. Master Corvus gave an icy stare, cold as winter stone, and nodded in grim consensus.

"We must send word to the northern garrison," Vaelen said, tracing a finger across the parchment map on the stone sarcophagus. "The Ashen Fortress is a grueling three-day hard ride north through the Jagged Peaks. We cannot afford any delay."

"And what of the assassin's weapon found in the courtyard?" Vivienne asked, nodding toward the slender blade resting upon the velvet cloth.

"A shadow-iron blade," Corvus warned in his gravelly voice. "An enchanted metal forged in the subterranean abysses. Remember the ancient rule: shadow-iron dissolves into harmless vapor the instant it touches running water. Keep it dry, or its deadly power is lost forever."

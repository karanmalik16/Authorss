# Chapter 3: The River Crossing

Dawn broke over the churning rapids of the River Marrow. The morning mist hung thick between the willow trees as the royal vanguard took up positions along the rocky embankment.

Master Corvus stood at the vanguard, his face rigid with fury as the enemy warband appeared on the opposite shore. Master Corvus gripped the heavy greatsword with both hands, his white-knuckled right fingers tightening on the leather hilt as he prepared to strike. He raised the massive blade above his shoulder, ready to cleave the charging raiders.

Across the frothing shallows, a hooded infiltrator emerged from the reeds. The assassin drew twin shadow-iron daggers, leaped into the rushing waters of the River Marrow, and waded across the torrent with blades drawn, entirely undeterred by the churning spray.

Captain Vaelen signaled the archers to release their volley, shouting orders through the roar of the river. The battle for the ford had begun.
